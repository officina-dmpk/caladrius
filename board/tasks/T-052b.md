## Thread

- 2026-10-10 orchestrator: card created from the T-051 review findings.
- 2026-10-10 engine: items 1-3 done, to review.
  - **1, refusal**: `MAX_DOSE_EVALUATIONS` lowered from 10^8 to 10^7 (reason in its doc comment). Exact rule, now also in the `SuperpositionTooLarge` message: a superposition is refused if and only if it needs more than 10^7 evaluations of one dose at one time after the early exit (counted at each evaluation, so the refusal fires at evaluation 10^7 + 1); each (dose, time) pair is evaluated at most once, so doses x times <= 10^7 is never refused (10,000 doses x 1,000 times always runs). New message: "{doses} doses at {times} times need more than {limit} evaluations of one dose at one time (the doses that still contribute at each time, summed over the times; a regimen is refused only beyond {limit}, so doses x times at most {limit} always runs); give fewer times or fewer doses, or use the steady state (`tau` alone) for a long regular regimen". Worst case (pk2.oral_1, cl 2e-5, 10,000 doses x 1,001 times after the last dose, no dose ever dropped): refusal after 22.0 s in release at 10^8 before, 2.18 s in release and 5.9 s in debug at 10^7 now (ignored test `refusal_at_the_crate_limit_is_prompt`, `cargo test -p caladrius-models --release --lib -- --ignored refusal_at --nocapture`). No up-front bound: an exact cheap prediction of the count after the exit is not available (the criterion depends on the running sums). Note: the message lives in `crates/caladrius-models/src/error.rs` (one line changed), outside the card's allowed files, because the brief asked for the rule in the error message.
  - **2, timing test**: renamed `superposition_of_10000_doses_at_10000_times_evaluates_few_doses`; it asserts the deterministic count from `superpose_counted` (< 1% of the 10^8 pairs: 551,416 for pk2.oral_1 regular, 311,756 for pk1.oral_1) and still runs pk2 through `run` at the crate limit; wall clock printed in release only (62.8 ms and 18.0 ms; 65.4 ms and 18.5 ms before this card).
  - **3, cheaper exit test**: `exhausted` is tested at the first dose visited and then every `CHECK_EVERY` = 8 doses. Conservative for any spacing: a drop still happens only where the criterion holds at the dose tested, with that dose's remaining amount and elapsed time; spacing only delays a drop by at most 7 doses, summed exactly (comment in the module doc and on the constant). `the_early_exit_gives_the_plain_sum` now asserts per model that the exit fires (evaluations < the plain sum's (dose, time) pairs) on at least one case of each of the 8 models; all 8 fire (the old `n < doses x times / 2` threshold would have failed pk1.oral_1_lag and the two-compartment models, whose exit fires on fewer pairs).
