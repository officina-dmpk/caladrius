# T-025: units in NCA results (dose unit, derived units)

- **Role:** engine
- **State:** in progress
- **Depends on:** T-015

## Goal

Found by the Apothicaire v0 demo (apothicaire/agent/README.md): `nca.run` only learns the dose unit from a dose column, so CL/F and Vz/F come out in "mg/(h·ng/mL)" and an LLM then converts them itself, wrongly. Add to `NcaInput` (or `NcaOptions`) explicit `time_unit`, `conc_unit` and `dose_unit` as data (golden rule 5), with a small unit table (time: s, min, h, d; mass: ng, µg, mg, g; volume: mL, L; concentration = mass/volume) and the derived units of every result (AUC in conc·time, AUMC in conc·time², λz in 1/time, CL/F in volume/time after converting the dose mass to the concentration mass unit, Vz/F and Vss in volume, MRT in time, `.dn` values per dose unit), carried in `NcaResult` as a unit string per parameter and serialized. Inconsistent or unknown units give a readable error before any computation (friction 5 of `AGENTS.md` section 7). Default units when absent: unchanged behaviour (unitless) with a flag `units_missing`. The oracle numbers must not change (PKNCA cases are unitless).

## Allowed files

`crates/caladrius-nca/src/`, `crates/caladrius-nca/Cargo.toml`, `crates/caladrius-nca/tests/units.rs` (new), `crates/caladrius-engine/src/` (only to pass the three units through `nca.run` and keep the schema in sync), `specs/differences.md` (append).
