Add to `specs/models.md` a section "Two-compartment models" (ids `MOD-2C-*`), in your own words and formulas, from the allowed sources only (Gabrielsson & Weiner; Gibaldi & Perrier; PKNCA documentation where relevant; published papers), never the reference software's documentation unless Q-005 is answered (it is `pending`: do not open it):

1. The system (central and peripheral compartments, micro-constants k10, k12, k21, volumes Vc and Vp, clearances CL and Q), and the equivalent parameterisations (macro-constants A, B, α, β; CL, Vc, Q, Vp; k, k12, k21, V): the conversion formulas in both directions, their domains (α > β > 0 etc.) and what is an error.
2. Closed-form concentration for IV bolus, IV infusion (during and after), first-order absorption with and without lag (with the ka = α and ka = β limits and stable evaluation near them, like MOD-AB1-02/03), zero-order absorption. Give each as a sum of exponentials with explicit coefficients.
3. Derived quantities: distribution and elimination half-lives, AUC, AUMC, MRT, Vss, Vz (Vβ), Cmax/Tmax where closed (or say "numerical"); consistency with NCA (like MOD-SEC-03).
4. Partial derivatives with respect to each fitted parameter in the chosen primary parameterisation (needed by `caladrius-fit`, see T-030), or state which parameterisation makes them simplest.
5. Model ids for the catalogue (MOD-GEN-05): e.g. `pk2.iv_bolus`, `pk2.iv_infusion`, `pk2.oral_1`, `pk2.oral_1_lag`, `pk2.oral_0`, with the default parameterisation per id.
6. Numerics: overflow and the α ≈ β near-degenerate case; what the engine must refuse.
7. Open items for the oracle (OM-xx): which cases the oracle must produce (R `deSolve` ODE cross-checks, closed-form grids, a 256-bit independent evaluation as in T-009), and which textbook worked examples with page references can be used.

Every rule carries a status (`assumed` or `documented, untested`; nothing is `confirmed` yet) and its sources with page or section. No copied sentence.
