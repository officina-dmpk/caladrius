## Goal

Tests that pin what `specs/population.md` says the population fit returns, written from the spec and the oracle, not from the engine. A test that fails stays failing, with its numbers and the rule it checks; the engine is fixed in another card.

1. **The three covariance kinds on the interior exact cases** (`oracle/expected/pop/fit_exact/`, the cases T-168 marks interior): assert the SEs of `sandwich`, `r` and `s` on θ, Ω and ξ against the exact values the oracle stores, through `se_by_kind`. No SE tolerance exists (Q-026): measure the engine's worst error per kind, propose one named constant in `caladrius-testkit` with its measurement and margin, and write the proposal in the card's thread for the orchestrator to put in Q-026. The review measured 2.8e-5 at worst.
2. **RSE, correlations, eigenvalues and condition number** derived from the printed covariance: assert them against an independent recomputation in the test (POP-COV-04), not against the engine's own numbers.
3. **The two-stage start (POP-EST-05):** requests with no `initial`, `omega` or `error_init`:
   - one that succeeds, with `start` per parameter and `left_out` listed;
   - each error sentence of POP-EST-05 item 3, triggered on purpose.
4. **Statuses (POP-EST-03, -04):** every status reachable through the public request with a small dataset (`max_iterations` with a low K_max, `non_finite`, `inner_failed`, `singular`, `no_decrease` where reachable); `a_request_without_evaluate_only_fits` must assert its status.
5. **`bounds` and a fixed Ω (POP-EST-01, -02):** a fit with a bound that binds (`at_bound` flag, the value on the bound); a fit with a fixed diagonal ω and one with a fixed whole block (the fixed values come back exactly).
