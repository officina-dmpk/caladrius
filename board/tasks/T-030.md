With the reference runs' convergence settings, the gap is purely the one-sided increment: the forward-difference gradient is off by O(h·θ·∂²f/∂θ²), so the iteration stops at the zero of a biased gradient, a shift proportional to h times the residuals that no stopping rule removes: h = 1e-3 gives 40 beyond 1e-4 (worst 6.9e-4), h = 1e-4 gives 0 (worst 7.0e-5, thin), h = 1e-5 gives 0 (worst 1.1e-5, SE 3.5e-5), h = 1e-6 gives 0 (8.0e-6); central differences at h = 1e-3 give 0 (worst 6.4e-6) at twice the model evaluations. But the default fit is also the default stopping rule (relative WRSS decrease 1e-4, 50 iterations), and that dominates: with it, forward 1e-3 gives 168 beyond 1e-4 (worst 5.9e-3), and forward 1e-5, central 1e-3 and even exact analytic derivatives all give 149 beyond (worst 5.1e-3, SE 9.9e-3): the criterion stops while the parameters are still moving. So the cheapest single change for D-03 as written (reference convergence) is the increment 1e-3 → 1e-5 (one constant, same cost, rounding error ~eps/h = 1e-11); bringing the shipped default within 1e-4 needs in addition a tighter stopping rule (e.g. `relative_offset`, or a relative decrease of 1e-8 to 1e-10), and analytic derivatives as default (now available for every v1 model in (v, k)) would then be the cleanest since they cost nothing. Both 0.001 and 0.0001 are the `assumed` reference-software defaults of AGENTS.md section 6: changing them trades fidelity to the reference software for fidelity to the exact minimum, which is the orchestrator's (or the human's) call. Nothing changed.
