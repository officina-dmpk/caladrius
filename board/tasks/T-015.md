# T-015: caladrius-nca quality flags and review follow-ups

- **Role:** engine
- **State:** to review
- **Depends on:** T-013

## Goal

(1) Quality flags per `specs/nca.md` NCA-LZ-12b (and any other flag rules in the spec): a serializable list of flags on `NcaResult` (e.g. λz from fewer than N points, adjusted R² below a threshold, span shorter than a multiple of t½, percent extrapolated above a threshold, λz computed from replaced values, Clast from a replaced value), thresholds in `NcaOptions` with the spec defaults, flags never change a number. (2) T-013 review findings: an all-BLQ profile whose values were replaced gives NC for areas when no measured sample is positive (or a flag plus documented behaviour, your call, documented in the card); `tlag` reports `no_data_after_cleaning` / `single_point` instead of `no_rise` on empty or single-sample profiles; document that `exclude_replaced` acts on λz only and add its typo-rejection and round-trip serde tests.
