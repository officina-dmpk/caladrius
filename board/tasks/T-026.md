- **Role:** interface

## Goal

Step 5, second slice, from `specs/ux.md` (frictions 2, 3, 4, 6 and the required improvements): a "Model fit" analysis page read top to bottom: data (worksheet, subject), model picked by route and number of compartments with its diagram and equation shown (one compartment: IV bolus, IV infusion, first-order absorption with/without lag, zero-order input), dose, initial estimates (prefilled by `fit.initial_estimates`, editable; the predicted curve from `model.simulate` updates live while they change), weighting and options, then results: summary first (estimates with SE and CV%, both confidence intervals, flags with sentences, convergence status), the fit plot (observed and predicted, linear/semi-log) and residual plots (residuals vs time, vs predicted; weighted residuals), details on demand (matrices, eigenvalues, condition number, trace, partials, predicted table). All curves come from the engine (`fit.run` curve, `model.simulate`), never computed in L3. A "Model simulation" page can reuse the same components (model, parameters, times → curve). Snapshots for every new screen in light and dark, looked at before delivery.
