## Goal

Write `specs/nca.md`: the behaviour of non-compartmental analysis as Caladrius must implement it, in our own words and formulas, with every rule tagged `confirmed by oracle`, `documented, untested` or `assumed`, and sourced.

## Allowed files

`specs/nca.md`, `specs/sources.md` (list of sources consulted with their terms of use), `board/QUESTIONS.md` (append only), `ATTRIBUTION.md` (append only, if a dataset or text is reused).

## Specification

Cover at least: AUC by linear, log-linear and mixed (linear up / log down) trapezoid rules, including the handling of zero and BLQ concentrations, of missing values, of duplicated or unsorted times; λz estimation (point selection: best adjusted R², minimum number of points, exclusion of Cmax, user override), t½, AUC extrapolation to infinity (observed and predicted last concentration), %AUC extrapolated, AUMC, MRT, CL/F, Vz/F, Vss for IV data, Cmax, Tmax, Tlast, Clast, C0 back-extrapolation for IV bolus; dose normalisation and units; what each parameter is called in the reference software versus PKNCA, as a vocabulary table (names only, no copied definitions).

Sources allowed: Gabrielsson & Weiner, Gibaldi & Perrier, the PKNCA documentation and vignettes, published papers, and the public user documentation of the reference software after checking its terms of use (section 6 of `AGENTS.md`). No sentence copied, no screenshot.

## Completion criterion

`specs/nca.md` exists, every rule has a status tag and at least one source, the vocabulary table has one row per parameter in scope, and `specs/sources.md` records the terms-of-use check. The orchestrator reads the status counts in the report.
