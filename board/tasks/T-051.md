# T-051: superposition cost: early exit for exhausted doses (follow-up of T-049)

- **Role:** engine
- **State:** to review
- **Depends on:** T-049

## Goal

The reviewer of T-049 measured `superpose` and the regular regimen in `crates/caladrius-models/src/dosing.rs` at O(doses × times) with no early exit: pk2 regular 10,000 doses × 1,000 times 2.6 s, 10,000 × 10,000 26 s, pk1 schedule 10,000 × 10,000 12 s (release; longer in debug and wasm). Steady state is 0.01 s.

1. Sort the doses once; for each evaluation time, skip the doses whose elapsed time exceeds a bound after which their contribution is below the double-precision floor relative to the running sum (for example a few hundred mean lives of the slowest exponent, derived from the model's rates, not a constant), or accumulate in dose order and stop when the remaining doses cannot change the sum; prove with a test that the result is unchanged at `MODEL_VALUES` on every md oracle case (881/881 stays green) and that the 10,000 × 10,000 case runs in under 1 s in release.
2. If the bound cannot be made exact for the AUC series, keep the exact path for AUC and apply the exit to concentrations only; say so in the card.
3. Optionally a readable refusal above a documented product doses × times (golden rule 6: never hang), stated in `specs/models.md` by the reader afterwards.

## Allowed files

`crates/caladrius-models/src/`, its unit tests, the card. Not the oracle, not the oracle tests.

  - **What changed**: new module `crates/caladrius-models/src/superpose.rs` (the superposition moved out of `dosing.rs`): doses sorted once (stable, by time), prefix sums of the amounts, and at each time a binary search for the doses given by then, visited from the newest back with an early exit; the visited contributions are then added oldest first from the area of the dropped doses, so with nothing dropped the result is the plain sum bit for bit. `Terms::dose_at` now evaluates each kernel once for both the concentration and the area (same sums, same order). New refusal `ModelError::SuperpositionTooLarge` above `MAX_DOSE_EVALUATIONS` = 10^8 evaluations of one dose at one time (counted after the early exit; any regimen with doses x times <= 10^8 is never refused), for the reader to state in `specs/models.md` with this message: "{doses} doses at {times} times need more than {limit} evaluations ...; give fewer times or fewer doses, or use the steady state".
  - **Bound, derived from the rates**: per unit dose and mode (weight w, rate λ, over V), Φ <= B and the tail ∫ₛ^∞ Φ <= B_tail, both non-increasing in s: bolus B = e^(−λs), B_tail = B/λ; zero-order input B = e^(−λ(s − T_max)) ((1 − e^−x)/x <= 1), B_tail = B/λ; first order B = ka·s·e^(−ms), m = min(λ, ka) (mean value theorem on (e^−λs − e^−ka·s)/(ka − λ)), from s >= 1/m, B_tail = ka·e^(−ms)(s/m + 1/m²). The doses not yet visited (total amount R, elapsed s of the newest of them) are dropped when R·Σ|w|B/V < 2^−62 of the concentration already summed and R·Σ|w|B_tail/V < 2^−62 of the area already summed (or R = 0, or both bounds have underflowed to exactly 0, as the kernel then has); their area is replaced by the area to infinity R·Σ w/(λV). Conservative: the change is below 2^−62 of the result, a thousandth of the last bit, so the AUC series uses the exit too (item 2 not needed). Expected zeros stay exact zeros (no comparison against an empty sum).
