# Public datasets

Both files are generated by `oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R` from the `datasets` package of R 4.5.2 and are not edited by hand. Column format (header on the first line, comma separated, LF line endings):

| column | meaning | unit |
|---|---|---|
| `subject` | integer subject code, as in R | |
| `time` | sampling time since the dose | h |
| `conc` | measured concentration | mg/L |
| `dose` | absolute dose given to that subject at time 0, repeated on every row of the subject | mg |

Numbers are written with 15 significant digits, so R and Rust read exactly the same decimal text.

## `theoph.csv`

- **Content:** 12 subjects, 11 samples each (132 rows), oral theophylline. Every subject has a sample at time 0 (`conc` may be non-zero there).
- **Origin:** the `Theoph` data frame of the R `datasets` package. The R documentation attributes the data to a study by Dr. Robert Upton reported in Boeckmann, Sheiner and Beal (1994), *NONMEM Users Guide, Part V*.
- **Conversion:** R stores the dose in mg/kg and the weight in kg. The `dose` column is their product, `Dose * Wt`, in mg (so clearance comes out as CL/F in L/h and volume as Vz/F in L). Rows are sorted by subject then time.
- **License:** distributed as part of R ("Part of R 4.5.2", R is GPL-2 or GPL-3). See `ATTRIBUTION.md` and question Q-004 in `board/QUESTIONS.md`.

## `indometh.csv`

- **Content:** 6 subjects, 11 samples each (66 rows), intravenous indometacin. There is no sample at time 0 (first sample at 0.25 h), so an IV bolus analysis needs a back-extrapolated C0.
- **Origin:** the `Indometh` data frame of the R `datasets` package. The R documentation cites Kwan, Breault, Umbenhauer, McMahon and Duggan (1976), *Journal of Pharmacokinetics and Biopharmaceutics* 4, 255-280, and Davidian and Giltinan (1995), *Nonlinear Models for Repeated Measurement Data*, section 5.2.4.
- **Dose is a test constant:** the R documentation gives no dose. The `dose` column is set to 25 mg for every subject. Results that do not involve the dose (concentrations, times, AUC, AUMC, lambda_z, half-life, MRT, C0) do not depend on this choice; CL, Vz and Vss scale with it. It is a convention shared by the R script and the Rust tests, not a claim about the original study.
- **License:** distributed as part of R (GPL-2 or GPL-3), as above.

## `synthetic_lz.csv`

- **Content:** 4 hand-made oral profiles (subjects 1 to 4), 9 samples each at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h, concentration 0 at time 0, `dose` 100 mg (a test constant). Tmax is at 1 h for every subject; the 6 samples after it are the eligible terminal-phase points, so the candidate windows have 3 to 6 points.
- **Origin and license:** made up for this project (task T-005), no external source; same license as the repository. The file is read, never written, by `oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R`.
- **Purpose:** the public profiles do not separate the competing readings of the terminal-phase rule (`specs/nca.md`, open items O-01 and O-02). Each subject was built to separate them. Subject 1 is profile D1 and subject 2 profile D2 of worked example W6 of that file; subjects 3 and 4 were found by a numerical search (a throwaway script, not versioned) and rounded to 3 or 4 decimals, then checked with PKNCA itself.
- **Cases:** `synthetic_lz` (PKNCA's explicit options of the other cases, factor `adj.r.squared.factor` 1e-4) and `synthetic_lz_f1e3` (the same with the factor 1e-3, which checks that the factor reaches the selection). 4 subjects x 29 parameters = 116 values each, 21 of them NA (subject 2 has no terminal phase, so every parameter that needs one is NA).

| subject | profile | points chosen by PKNCA (factor 1e-4) | other readings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D1 of W6 | 3 (lambda_z 0.158728533) | bonus reading: 6 (0.157039187, which PKNCA gives with factor 1e-3) |
| 2 | D2 of W6 | none (lambda_z NA) | positive filter first: 6 |
| 3 | found | 4 (0.242277436) | bonus 5, best adjusted R squared alone 3, longest window 6 |
| 4 | found, erratic tail | 4 (0.024811852) | positive filter first: 5 (0.015070393) |

`min.hl.r.squared` (0.9) does not blank lambda_z in PKNCA 0.12.1: subject 4 has an adjusted R squared of -0.216 and a lambda_z. Independent re-implementations of the four readings and the check that PKNCA's answer is the tolerance one live in `crates/caladrius-testkit/tests/synthetic_discrimination.rs`.

## Edge-case profiles (`edge_*.csv`, task T-012)

Hand-made profiles for the rules of `specs/nca.md` that the public datasets do not exercise (open item O-17). Same columns as above; made up for this project, no external source, same license as the repository; read, never written, by `oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R`. An **empty `conc` field is a missing value** (the testkit loader reads it as NaN). Unlike the public cases, these are given to PKNCA as they are (no point is added, `preprocessing` in each options file says so), and the cases whose first sample may be removed use the engine hint `start = none`, because PKNCA does not invent a concentration at the dose time. Each options file has a `case_note`, and an `engine` section with the settings PKNCA has no option for (`start`, `negative`); an infusion case has `infusion_duration`. Times are 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h unless said otherwise; doses are test constants.

| file | content | cases |
|---|---|---|
| `edge_blq.csv` | 6 oral profiles, 100 mg, with zeros: interior (subject 1, after Tmax; subject 4, in the terminal phase; subject 6, before Tmax), leading after the dose time (2, 5), trailing (3, 5) | `edge_blq_default` (first keep, middle drop, last keep: PKNCA's default written out), `edge_blq_keep` (all kept), `edge_blq_last_drop` (first keep, middle keep, last drop), `edge_blq_first_drop` (all dropped, so the dose-time sample goes and no AUC exists), `edge_blq_set` (first keep, middle replaced by 0.3, last by 0.05), `edge_blq_tmax` (before Tmax kept, from Tmax on dropped) |
| `edge_missing.csv` | 5 oral profiles, 100 mg, with empty concentrations (interior, at Tmax, last, two in one profile) | `edge_missing_drop` (dropped with their times), `edge_missing_replace` (replaced by 1) |
| `edge_negative.csv` | 2 oral profiles, 100 mg: an interior negative (-0.3 at 4 h) and a trailing one (-0.2 at 24 h) | `edge_negative_linear`: linear rule, because lin up/log down gives NaN areas with a negative concentration in PKNCA; engine hint `negative = allow` |
| `edge_oral.csv` | 6 oral profiles, 20 to 250 mg: no lag (1, 6 starts at once), lags of 1 and 2 h (2, 3), a profile that only declines (4, so no Tlag), a pre-dose baseline (5); subject 3 has Tmax at 8 h, so only 2 points follow it and lambda_z is not estimable | `edge_oral`: Tlag, MRT to Tlast, plain Vss, dose-normalised values, percent AUMC extrapolated |
| `edge_iv.csv` | 3 IV bolus profiles, 25 mg, times 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, always with a record at the dose time: observed C0 = 10 (1), a zero placeholder with decreasing first pair (2), a zero placeholder with a flat first pair, so C0 falls back to the first post-dose value (3) | `edge_iv`, `edge_iv_linear`: the three C0 methods, PKNCA's IV areas (`aucivlast`, `aucivall`, `aucivinf.*`) and percent back-extrapolated (`aucivpbext*`), MRT and Vss to Tlast, plain Vss |
| `edge_infusion.csv` | 3 profiles, 50, 100, 200 mg infused over 2 h (Cmax at the end of the infusion), times 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 | `edge_infusion`, `edge_infusion_linear` (`infusion_duration` 2): MRT and Vss corrected by half the duration, no terminal-phase point during the infusion |

PKNCA has no percent AUMC extrapolated: the script derives `aumcpext.obs` and `aumcpext.pred` as `100 * (1 - aumclast / aumcinf)` from PKNCA's own AUMC values (the options files say so, `derived_parameters`), and `crates/caladrius-testkit/tests/edge_consistency.rs` re-derives the relation independently. That test file also re-implements the BLQ and missing-value cleaning from the rules of `specs/nca.md`, integrates the cleaned profiles with the naive trapezoids and reproduces PKNCA's areas, and applies the MRT, Vss, dose-normalisation, percent-extrapolated, percent-back-extrapolated, C0 and Tlag relations to the expected values.

Facts found by running PKNCA 0.12.1 on these profiles (the oracle records them; none is a choice of ours):

- BLQ classes follow the rules of `specs/nca.md` DAT-06. A zero or a missing value that a policy replaced by a positive number is an ordinary point for the areas and for lambda_z (open item O-07: it is not excluded from the terminal phase), and `auclast`/`aumclast` integrate up to the last positive point after the replacement (so with trailing zeros replaced, `auclast` equals `aucall`). But PKNCA reports `tfirst`, `tlast` and `clast.obs` from the data before the replacement: a trailing zero replaced by 0.05 is not Tlast, and a missing value replaced by 1 is neither Tfirst nor Tlast (`edge_blq_set`, `edge_missing_replace`).
- With a negative concentration PKNCA warns, and `tlast`/`clast.obs` are the last non-zero point, so a trailing negative value is Tlast and Clast (`edge_negative_linear` subject 2); the terminal phase never uses a non-positive point. The lin-up/log-down areas are NaN.
- PKNCA's `vss.obs` and `vss.pred` exist for every route and equal CL times the uncorrected MRT (`vss.iv.*` subtract half the infusion duration).
- Tlag is the time of the sample before the first increase (0 when the profile rises at once, not available when it never rises).
- PKNCA percent back-extrapolated is `100 * (1 - AUC / AUC_iv)` with its IV area; zero when the record at the dose time is the observed C0.

## `fit/spec.csv` (task T-009)

The five-point IV bolus example of `specs/fit.md` section 11 (dose 100, times 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, concentrations 9.31, 7.92, 6.85, 4.31, 2.11), in the usual columns with subject 1. Made up for that specification (no source, same license as the repository). It is fitted by `oracle/scripts/fit_wls.R` with the five weightings; the uniform and `inv_y2` fits reproduce the hand-computed examples F2 and F3 of the specification, and `crates/caladrius-testkit/src/step3.rs` tests that. The Theoph fits leave out the sample at time 0 (the oral model predicts 0 there, which weights on predicted values cannot use); the Indometh fits use all samples.
