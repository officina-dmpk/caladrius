# Public datasets

Both files are generated by `oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R` from the `datasets` package of R 4.5.2 and are not edited by hand. Column format (header on the first line, comma separated, LF line endings):

| column | meaning | unit |
|---|---|---|
| `subject` | integer subject code, as in R | |
| `time` | sampling time since the dose | h |
| `conc` | measured concentration | mg/L |
| `dose` | absolute dose given to that subject at time 0, repeated on every row of the subject | mg |

Numbers are written with 15 significant digits, so R and Rust read exactly the same decimal text.

## `theoph.csv`

- **Content:** 12 subjects, 11 samples each (132 rows), oral theophylline. Every subject has a sample at time 0 (`conc` may be non-zero there).
- **Origin:** the `Theoph` data frame of the R `datasets` package. The R documentation attributes the data to a study by Dr. Robert Upton reported in Boeckmann, Sheiner and Beal (1994), *NONMEM Users Guide, Part V*.
- **Conversion:** R stores the dose in mg/kg and the weight in kg. The `dose` column is their product, `Dose * Wt`, in mg (so clearance comes out as CL/F in L/h and volume as Vz/F in L). Rows are sorted by subject then time.
- **License:** distributed as part of R ("Part of R 4.5.2", R is GPL-2 or GPL-3). See `ATTRIBUTION.md` and question Q-004 in `board/QUESTIONS.md`.

## `indometh.csv`

- **Content:** 6 subjects, 11 samples each (66 rows), intravenous indometacin. There is no sample at time 0 (first sample at 0.25 h), so an IV bolus analysis needs a back-extrapolated C0.
- **Origin:** the `Indometh` data frame of the R `datasets` package. The R documentation cites Kwan, Breault, Umbenhauer, McMahon and Duggan (1976), *Journal of Pharmacokinetics and Biopharmaceutics* 4, 255-280, and Davidian and Giltinan (1995), *Nonlinear Models for Repeated Measurement Data*, section 5.2.4.
- **Dose is a test constant:** the R documentation gives no dose. The `dose` column is set to 25 mg for every subject. Results that do not involve the dose (concentrations, times, AUC, AUMC, lambda_z, half-life, MRT, C0) do not depend on this choice; CL, Vz and Vss scale with it. It is a convention shared by the R script and the Rust tests, not a claim about the original study.
- **License:** distributed as part of R (GPL-2 or GPL-3), as above.

## `synthetic_lz.csv`

- **Content:** 4 hand-made oral profiles (subjects 1 to 4), 9 samples each at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h, concentration 0 at time 0, `dose` 100 mg (a test constant). Tmax is at 1 h for every subject; the 6 samples after it are the eligible terminal-phase points, so the candidate windows have 3 to 6 points.
- **Origin and license:** made up for this project (task T-005), no external source; same license as the repository. The file is read, never written, by `oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R`.
- **Purpose:** the public profiles do not separate the competing readings of the terminal-phase rule (`specs/nca.md`, open items O-01 and O-02). Each subject was built to separate them. Subject 1 is profile D1 and subject 2 profile D2 of worked example W6 of that file; subjects 3 and 4 were found by a numerical search (a throwaway script, not versioned) and rounded to 3 or 4 decimals, then checked with PKNCA itself.
- **Cases:** `synthetic_lz` (PKNCA's explicit options of the other cases, factor `adj.r.squared.factor` 1e-4) and `synthetic_lz_f1e3` (the same with the factor 1e-3, which checks that the factor reaches the selection). 4 subjects x 29 parameters = 116 values each, 21 of them NA (subject 2 has no terminal phase, so every parameter that needs one is NA).

| subject | profile | points chosen by PKNCA (factor 1e-4) | other readings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D1 of W6 | 3 (lambda_z 0.158728533) | bonus reading: 6 (0.157039187, which PKNCA gives with factor 1e-3) |
| 2 | D2 of W6 | none (lambda_z NA) | positive filter first: 6 |
| 3 | found | 4 (0.242277436) | bonus 5, best adjusted R squared alone 3, longest window 6 |
| 4 | found, erratic tail | 4 (0.024811852) | positive filter first: 5 (0.015070393) |

`min.hl.r.squared` (0.9) does not blank lambda_z in PKNCA 0.12.1: subject 4 has an adjusted R squared of -0.216 and a lambda_z. Independent re-implementations of the four readings and the check that PKNCA's answer is the tolerance one live in `crates/caladrius-testkit/tests/synthetic_discrimination.rs`.
